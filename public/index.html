How to play

  • 👆Tap a tile to paint it your color. Each tap costs 1 energy. Energy refills on its own.
  • 🥷Steal other colors by painting over them. Fresh paint is protected for about 1 second.
  • 🎁Grab glowing tiles for power-ups: 💥 Splat (paint 3x3), 🧊 Freeze (everyone else), ⚡ Rush (free taps), 🔀 Swap (trade colors with the leader).
  • 🔥In the last 10 seconds it's FRENZY: energy refills twice as fast and more power-ups drop.
  • 🏆When the clock hits zero, most tiles wins. 2 to 8 players.
